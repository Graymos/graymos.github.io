Security Operations

My consulting work includes system hardening, vulnerability assessment, security audits, patching, configuration management, and monitoring. I have deployed SIEM and IDS/IPS visibility with Wazuh and documented findings and procedures.

Automation and Development

I use Python, C, C++, and Bash for programming and automation, with Ansible, SQL/MariaDB, Git/GitHub, and exposure to Google Cloud Platform. I enjoy turning repeatable technical work into documented, maintainable processes.

I maintain a personal Proxmox environment for virtualization and experimentation. It gives me a safe place to run services such as Wazuh, explore cloud environments, practice administration, and test defensive configurations.

Research and Consulting

My consulting, research, and competition experience taught me to learn quickly, work across disciplines, and communicate technical findings. At Veracity Nuclear, I support secure virtual infrastructure and am developing a SOAR workflow with n8n. In 2025, I was selected from applicants across the country for the INSuRE Summer Workshop, operated by the CAE Community. Working with students and faculty from other institutions under NSA problem mentors, I contributed to Windows source-to-sink analysis and automated binary-analysis research. I was also part of Tennessee Tech's team that reached the finals of the Cyber Resiliency and Measurement Challenge, hosted by the Naval Surface Warfare Center Dahlgren Division (NSWCDD). We trained a localized AI model to evaluate descriptions of an organization's networks, systems, services, and physical environment; I worked on the scoring that turned software, hardware, physical, and operational risk information into a cyber-resiliency score and recommendations.
