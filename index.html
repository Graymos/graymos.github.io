CRAM Competitor

I and a team of four others were accepted into and finalists of the Cyber Resiliency and Measurement Challenge (CRAM) hosted by Naval Surface Warfare Center Dahlgren Division (NSWCDD). We developed algorithms to measure a system’s cyber defensive resilience against full-spectrum threats and to calculate probability of failure across threat profiles. Tennessee Technological University's Cyber Education and Research and Outreach Center (CEROC) sponsored our travel to Dahlgren, Virginia, where our resiliency model used localized AI training, data extraction, normalization, and scoring of software, hardware, physical, and operational risks.
